Clinical Problem & Solution

Eliminating Bias in Hemodynamic Assessment

Conventional Capillary Refill Time (CRT) fails in darker skin tones due to epidermal melanin attenuation. ShockGuard bridges this vital gap with engineering precision.

⚡

Multi-Spectral Melanin Calibration

Pre-blanch optical reflectance measures baseline melanin index ($M$). Dynamically scales ring LED PWM illumination (55% to 98%) and adapts contrast thresholds to achieve uniform sensitivity across all skin phototypes.

⚙️

Motor-Free Spring Repeatability

Eliminates battery-draining servo motors. Pulled down via a radial knob to cock, and fired via a single mechanical release button. A precision travel stop guarantees a strict 4.0 mm calibrated displacement.

🩺

Dual-Modality Cross-Validation

Synchronously fuses optical blanching CRT with real-time Peripheral Perfusion Index (PPI) from a flush MAX30102 sensor. Delivers a composite Triage Risk Score for hypovolemic and septic shock.

🧠

Edge Computing & Display

Dual-core ESP32-S3 (240MHz, 8MB PSRAM) executes real-time exponential curve fitting ($CRT_{90} = 2.303\tau$) and renders actionable Green/Amber/Red alert tiers directly on a 0.96" OLED screen.

🔍

Locked-Exposure Macro Optics

Custom OV2640 macro barrel at 13mm working distance. Automatically locks AGC/AEC/AWB before release, guaranteeing that pixel intensity changes represent true dermal reperfusion rather than camera gain.

📦

FDM-Optimized Split Chassis

Serviceable two-piece shell in PETG/ASA. Removable electronics tray, clear service paths, captive fasteners, and dedicated floor battery door for effortless maintenance.

Embedded Hardware & Firmware

Electronics Architecture & BOM

Designed around the high-performance ESP32-S3 microcontroller with 16MB Flash, 8MB PSRAM, and integrated power management.

🔌

Power & Battery Subsystem

1S 3.7V 450mAh LiPo battery (502238 envelope). SMT USB Type-C connector with TP4056 linear charger, DW01A protection IC, FS8205A dual MOSFET, and low-noise AP2112K-3.3 LDO (600mA).

📷

Optical DVP Interface

8-bit parallel digital video port (DVP) connecting the OV2640 macro camera to ESP32-S3 DMA channels. Captures 160×120 QQVGA frames at 30+ FPS for high temporal resolution during reperfusion.

💡

Ring Illumination Board

Custom 14×9 mm PCB with 6 high-CRI 4000K LEDs driven by a DMG2302 N-MOSFET via a 20kHz PWM timer. Eliminates optical flicker and maintains high SNR across Fitzpatrick IV–VI.
